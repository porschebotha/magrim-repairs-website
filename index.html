About Magrim Repairs

Magrim Repairs is a Windhoek-based specialist in car rim repair and restoration. We bring damaged rims back to life — quickly, safely and at a fair price.

Our customers trust us because we focus on doing the job properly. Every rim is carefully inspected, repaired with the right tools and techniques, and finished to a standard we are proud of. Whether your rim is bent from a pothole, cracked, or scratched by a curb, we treat it as if it were our own.

  • Professional, reliable workmanship
